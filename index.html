ONE PORTABLE ARCHIVE

Application and emails together.

New messages are added, duplicates skipped and Gmail labels merged. Existing messages are never removed just because they are absent from a new export.

Structure: index.html, assets/ and archived_emails/. The latter contains all accounts, year/month HTML messages, attachments and catalogs. Copy or merge account folders into archived_emails/ and click Rebuild index to rebuild indexes, without the MBOX.

Keep this tab open while importing. Use only one writer per archive, including across synced devices.

Reading an indexed archive works by opening this index.html offline. Chrome/Edge require one-time permission to this app folder for import/reindex. No server or internet connection is needed.

The per-message limit is 64 MiB. Oversized or damaged messages are reported as errors; the source stays untouched.
